# Tibetan Pronunciation Reference (Standard Central Tibetan)

Source: tibetanlanguage.school — for personal reference only, not affiliated.

---

## Script & basics

- **Script:** དབུ་ཅན་ *uchän* — left-to-right; no spaces between words, syllables separated by **ཚེག་ tshek** (small dot ་)
- **30 consonants** (གསལ་བྱེད་ *säche*) + **4 vowels** (དབྱངས་ *yang*) = 34 letters
- Consonants carry a **default "a"** when standalone (no vowel letter attached)
- **Script is tonal** — 2 tones: high (unmarked) and low (underline in phonetic: a̲)

---

## The 30 consonants — 8 rows

Each cell: `tibetan — wylie — phonetic — tone`

| Row | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-----|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ཀ་སྡེ་ | ཀ ka ka high | ཁ kha k͟ha high | ག ga k͟ha **low** | ང nga ng**a̲** low |
| 2 ཅ་སྡེ་ | ཅ ca cha high | ཆ cha chha high | ཇ ja chha **low** | ཉ nya ny**a̲** low |
| 3 ཏ་སྡེ་ | ཏ ta ta high | ཐ tha tha high | ད da tha **low** | ན na n**a̲** low |
| 4 པ་སྡེ་ | པ pa pa high | ཕ pha pha high | བ ba pha **low** | མ ma m**a̲** low |
| 5 ཙ་སྡེ་ | ཙ tsa tsa high | ཚ tsha tsha high | ཛ dza dz**a̲** low | ཝ wa w**a̲** low |
| 6 ཞ་སྡེ་ | ཞ zha sh**a̲** low | ཟ za s**a̲** low | འ 'a ɦ**a̲** low | ཡ ya y**a̲** low |
| 7 ར་སྡེ་ | ར ra r**a̲** low | ལ la l**a̲** low | ཤ sha sha high | ས sa sa high |
| 8 ཧ་སྡེ་ | ཧ ha ha high | ཨ a a high (null) | | |

**Aspiration** (burst of air) = marked with h suffix in phonetic (kha, tha, etc.)
**Rows 1–4 pattern:** unaspirated-high / aspirated-high / aspirated-low / nasal-low

## The 4 vowels

Attach above or below a consonant; always inherit consonant's tone.

| Letter | Name | Sound | Position |
|--------|------|-------|----------|
| ི | གི་གུ་ *khikhu* | i (ee in "see") | above |
| ུ | ཞབས་ཀྱུ་ *shabkyu* | u (oo in "too") | below |
| ེ | འགྲེང་བུ་ *drengbu* | e (ey in "hey") | above |
| ོ | ན་རོ་ *naro* | o (o in "so") | above |

Default vowel = "a" (unwritten). No vowel letter = consonant + "a".

## Syllable structure — 7 positions

```
[prefix] [superscript]
         [MAIN LETTER]  [vowel]  [suffix]  [post-suffix]
         [subscript]
```

1. **Prefix** (སྔོན་འཇུག་ *ngönjuk*) — left of main; 5 possible: ག་ད་བ་མ་འ་
2. **Superscript** (མགོ་ཅན་ *gochän*) — above main; stacked
3. **Main letter** (མིང་གཞི་ *mingshi*) — core; any of 30
4. **Subscript** (འདོགས་ཅན་ *dokchän*) — below main; stacked
5. **Vowel** (དབྱངས་ *yang*) — above or below main
6. **Suffix** (རྗེས་འཇུག་ *jenjuk*) — right of main; 10 possible: ག་ང་ད་ན་བ་མ་འ་ར་ལ་ས་
7. **Post-suffix** (ཡང་འཇུག་ *yangjuk*) — right of suffix; 2 possible: ད་ས་ (ད་ obsolete)

## Key pronunciation rules (10 basics)

1. **Suffix letters** lose default "a", tone, aspiration → strong/weak forms
2. **Prefix, superscript, post-suffix** are usually **silent**
3. **Prefix/superscript** cause ག་ཇ་ད་བ་ to become **unaspirated** (and often voiced)
4. **Prefix/superscript** cause nasal letters (ང་ཉ་ན་མ་) + ཡ་ to shift to **high tone**
5. **Subscript ར་** forms "tr" with some main letters, silent with others
6. **Subscript ཡ་** → "-y" with ཀ་སྡེ་; "ch" with པ་སྡེ་
7. **Subscript ལ་** → high-tone "l"
8. **Subscript ཝ་** is silent
9. Rules combine: བསྒྲུབས་ → *drup* (rules 1+2+3+5)
10. Minor rules and exceptions covered in Unit 3+

## Prefix effects in detail

**Prefix letters:** ག་ད་བ་མ་འ་ (all silent by default)

| Main letter type | Effect |
|-----------------|--------|
| ང་ཉ་ན་མ་ ཡ་ (nasal + ya) | tone → **high** |
| ག་ཇ་ད་བ་ | → unaspirated + often voiced (low tone) |
| ཞ་ཟ་ | some speakers voice (Zhu/Zi lects); course teaches Shu/Si |
| All others | **no effect** |

**Exception དབ-:** prefix ད་ + main བ་  
- vowel u/o → silent onset, high tone: དབུ་ → *u*  
- vowel a/i/e → w-, high tone

## Suffix letters

**10 suffix letters:** ག་ང་ད་ན་བ་མ་འ་ར་ལ་ས་

| Type | Letters | Strong form |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Unchanged | ང་ མ་ | ng, m |
| Variable (strong/silent) | ག་ན་བ་ར་ལ་ | k/–, n/–, p/–, r/–(lengthens vowel), l/–(lengthens) |
| Silent | ད་ ས་ འ་ | silent (ད་ rarely "t" in formal speech) |

**Vowel raising** — suffix letters ད་ན་ལ་ས་ raise vowels:
- a → ä, u → ü, o → ö

---

## Post-suffix letters

ད་ (obsolete) and ས་ — both **completely silent**.  
Final ས་ can cause a sharply falling tone.

---

## Dialect notes

- **Kha lects** (this course): ག་ཇ་ད་བ་ as aspirated (k͟ha, chha, tha, pha)
- **Ga lects** (older, more common): same letters as ga, ja, da, ba
- Extended Wylie + THL transcription use Ga lect conventions — be aware
